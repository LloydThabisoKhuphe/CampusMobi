# CampusMobi - personas and scenarios 

Persona 1: Sipho (Undergraduate Student)
Age: 20
Occupation: Second-Year Computer Science Student
Goal: Manage academic schedules and access campus services easily.
Scenario

Sipho logs into CampusMobi using his student number and checks his academic calendar before classes. He receives a notification reminding him about an upcoming lecture and uses the campus map to locate a new lab venue.

Persona 2: Ayanda (First-Year Student)
Age: 18
Occupation: First-Year Student
Goal: Navigate campus without getting lost.
Scenario

Ayanda opens the campus map feature to search for her lecture hall and residence. She clicks on different campus buildings to view information and directions, helping her confidently move around campus during orientation week.

Persona 3: Lwazi (Campus Staff Member)
Age: 41
Occupation: Security Officer
Goal: Verify student identities quickly and efficiently.
Scenario

Lwazi uses CampusMobi to scan a student’s digital QR code at the entrance of a campus event. The system immediately displays the student’s details, allowing quick and secure verification.

Persona 4: Naledi (Busy Student)
Age: 22
Occupation: Final-Year Accounting Student
Goal: Stay updated with classes, assignments, and events.
Scenario

Naledi adds assignment deadlines and study group meetings to her CampusMobi calendar. The system automatically sends reminders before each event, helping her manage her time effectively during exam season.

Persona 5: Mr. Dlamini (Campus Administrator)
Age: 45
Occupation: Student Services Administrator
Goal: Monitor student engagement and improve campus services.
Scenario

Mr. Dlamini reviews student engagement statistics on CampusMobi to see which services students use the most. He also reads feedback submitted by students to identify areas where the platform can be improved.

Persona 6: Zanele (Tech-Savvy Student)
Age: 21
Occupation: Information Systems Student
Goal: Experience a digital campus payment system.
Scenario

Zanele uses the demo wallet feature to simulate adding funds and making payments for campus services. She regularly checks her wallet balance and transaction history to track her simulated spending
